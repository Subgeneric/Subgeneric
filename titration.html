Reading the curve: Before the equivalence point, pH rises gradually while acid remains. Near the equivalence point, pH changes sharply and the indicator changes color. After the equivalence point, excess NaOH makes the solution increasingly basic. Switch to CH₃COOH to see the equivalence point shift above pH 7 for a weak acid–strong base titration.
